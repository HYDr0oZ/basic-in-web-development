More Than Just a Rental Shop

At Alpine Ascent, we believe that bad gear shouldn't ruin a good snow day. Founded in 2018 by former competitive skiers, we set out to solve the problem of outdated, poorly maintained rental equipment.

We stock only the latest models from top-tier brands, freshly tuned and waxed before every single rental. Whether you're a beginner finding your footing or an expert chasing powder, we have the perfect setup for you.
